Extrusion slides accomplish the same purpose as channel slides, the conversion of rotational energy into linear.
But they accomplish it is a different way, instead of pre-made channels designed entirely to slide onto one another, it works with multipurpose extrusion, this simple fact comes with its own up and down sides.

Note: extrusion slides have a issue of flexing under load while being cheap and lightweight, thus you will see these attributes in a lot of these.

Some examples of extrusion slides include:

REV Robotics 15mm Linear Motion Kit:

Link:
- https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-45-1507/
( the parts included are for the 15mm Extrusions by rev, they seem to be sold separately )

Advantages
- Great official documentation provided by REV for rigging
- Interfaces with REV building system, Can be a downside if your using GoBILDA's or other build systems
- Cheap
Disadvantages:
- Needs to be tweaked out of the box to get its full potential*
- Flexes under load
- Rev as a brand suffers from supply chain issues (could be a issue for a last minute purchase)

OpenBuilds Mini V Gantry kit

Link:
- https://us.openbuilds.com/mini-v-gantry-kit/

Advantages:
- Very sturdy
- Interfaces with most other build systems (including GoBILDA!!)
- Moderately priced
- High precision
Disadvantages:
- Not designed for multi-stage lifts* (can be modified)
- Super Heavy
- Only M3 and M5 Bolts available
- Currently Suffering Supply chain issues

Generalization:
Typically extrusion slides will be lighter & cheaper than channel slides, while being less steady and higher effort to implement.
Out of the options presented I believe a self made system with standard aluminum extrusion is the best option, as it suffers the same draw backs as most other systems while being much cheaper than alternatives.
